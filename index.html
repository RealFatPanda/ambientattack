AmbientAttack: Black-Box Attacks on Automatic Speech Recognition via Acoustic Injection

This paper has been submitted to ICASSP 207 for peer reviewing.

Over-the-air Attack

We present two audio samples evaluated using Wav2Vec. For each sample, we demonstrate six over-the-air attacks under different configurations, including three angles (45°, 90°, and 180°) with two distances (40 cm and 80 cm). For comparison, we also conduct an over-the-air attack using only the clean speech, with the distance between the device playing the speech and the recorder fixed at 40 cm.

  1. Ground Truth transcription 01: Lock the front door after everyone has left the house this evening.
  2. Ground Truth transcription 02: Navigate to one hundred twenty-five West Main Street using the fastest route.
